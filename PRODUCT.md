# PRODUCT.md

## Register

Product UI / tool

## Target Users

Individuals

## Purpose

Searchable conversation memory

## Brand Personality

Minimalist and futuristic

## Anti-References

Generic, creamy, and boring

## Accessibility Needs

No specific requirements

## Strategic Design Principles

- Prioritize clarity and speed for capturing and searching conversations
- Minimalist interface with strong focus on utility
- Avoid visual clutter and unnecessary decoration
- Ensure the interface feels modern and forward-looking

## Notes

- Avoid SaaS-cream and generic dashboard tropes
- Focus on information density and ease of navigation
- Brand should feel distinct from typical productivity tools
